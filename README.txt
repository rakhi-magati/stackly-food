Stackly food app (HTML + CSS + JavaScript only)

OPEN: unzip, then double-click index.html.

FILES
  index.html  pages and sections
  style.css   design and animations
  art.js      the food illustrations (shaded SVG dishes)
  app.js      cart, coupons, payment, tracking, tabs, animations

REAL PHOTOS (optional, recommended)
  Put your own food photos in an "images" folder next to index.html, named:
  margherita-pizza.jpg  smash-burger.jpg  chicken-biryani.jpg  sushi-platter.jpg
  paneer-wrap.jpg  creamy-pasta.jpg  street-tacos.jpg  tonkotsu-ramen.jpg
  garden-salad.jpg  berry-ice-cream.jpg  loaded-fries.jpg  fruit-bowl.jpg
  Square photos work best. Any missing photo falls back to the drawn dish automatically.

TABS
  Home        deals slider, top-picks slider, full menu with filters
  Restaurants featured slider, filter + sort, dish sliders, table booking
  Offers      deals slider, flash-deal countdown, 6 coupon tickets, FAQ
  My orders   stats, Active/Past/Table bookings tabs, live tracking, star ratings, receipts
  About       story, stats, values, journey, reviews slider, FAQ, contact form
COUPONS: STACKLY50, FREEDEL, BIGEAT, FIRST100, WEEKEND15, PARTY25, STACK10 (Stack builder).
Contact details, reviews and restaurant info are sample text. Replace them with your own.
Payments are a simulation. Orders and favourites are saved in your browser.
